The Artist
Profile

A hand trained in cloth,
and in colour.

Born in Kosamba, Surat district, to a drawing teacher, Rajnikant C. Chauhan is a multi-disciplinary artist. He spent fifteen years as a free-hand sketch artist in the fashion and textile industries of Surat — then, in 2001, left an eleven-year post to give himself wholly to painting and sculpture. That leap carried him to solo exhibitions across India and to the M. P. Birla Art Gallery in London (2008 & 2009), where twenty-two canvases and ten carved-wood sculptures were shown.

He is also the founder of Square Art Group, a collective of South Gujarat artists. Today, the same artistic knowledge anchors a Digital Textile Design practice for the fashion industry.

Cloth and canvas are not two disciplines. They are one language, spoken in two rooms.

Current Practice · Digital Textile Design

Where the studio
meets the factory.

Today, this profile is anchored firmly in the fashion and textile industry. Drawing on more than a decade of hand-drawn pattern work and a fine-artist's eye for colour, Rajnikant works as a Digital Textile Designer — translating instinct into repeat, colourway and print-ready artwork for the digital press and the mill floor alike.

The fine-art past is not left behind; it is the foundation. Every motif carries the same discipline he once carried into a gallery — only now it lands on fabric worn every day.

The gallery trained my eye. The industry gives that eye a purpose.

Sculpture — Nehru Art Gallery
Sculpture

Form Carved
in Wood

Alongside the canvas, a parallel body of work in wood — ten sculptures travelled to London in 2009, and carving has remained part of the daily studio practice. Figurative and geometric, they share the same discipline as the paintings: a line eventually trusted enough to stand in three dimensions.

A line asked to remain — drawn in the grain instead of paint.

With Shri S. H. Raza — Paris
A Meeting in Paris

In the Company
of Raza

At his residence in Paris, he met Syed Haider Raza — one of India's most celebrated modern painters — and presented his paintings to the master. It remains a defining moment in a life devoted to art.

A gift of canvas offered to a legend, between two artists who never needed to explain.

Letter of Appreciation — Narendra Modi
Recognition

A Letter That
Mattered

A gesture of recognition that carries weight: Narendra Modi — then Chief Minister of Gujarat and now Prime Minister of India — acknowledged the work in writing. The letter speaks to the persistence behind a practice that moves between canvas, cloth and carved wood.

To be seen by a leader is to know the hours at the easel were worth every one of them.

Square Art Group — Opening View
Founder

Square Art Group

Rajnikant is the founder of Square Art Group, a collective of South Gujarat artists. In January 2010, a group of thirty-six artists exhibited at Anayas Art Gallery, Ahmedabad under the group's aegis — a series of original modern paintings, landscapes, foil-emboss work and sculptures, opened without a single rupee of sponsorship or donation. The group brought promising young artists and first-year fine-arts students onto the same floor as seasoned hands, and honoured senior artists with its Kaladhar Award — a commitment to craft over commerce that still defines the philosophy.
